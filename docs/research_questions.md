# Research questions

## RQ1 — Individual ecological resistance
How heterogeneous is baseline-relative mammary microbiome displacement among individual quarters and cows after dry-cow intervention?

## RQ2 — Ecological determinants
Which pre-intervention ecological characteristics are associated with greater resistance, and do those associations remain after referencing each treated quarter to the untreated quarter in the same cow?

## RQ3 — Multidimensional resistance
Do compositional stability, diversity stability, taxon retention, and dominance stability describe one common resistance axis or distinct ecological dimensions?

## Translational extension
The framework is designed for later comparison of conventional intramammary antibiotics with narrow-spectrum therapeutics, including antimicrobial peptides, where equivalent pathogen clearance could be evaluated alongside ecological collateral perturbation.
