# Manuscript working outline

## Working title
**Individual-level ecological resistance of the bovine mammary microbiome to dry-cow antimicrobial perturbation**

## Introduction
- Mammary microbial ecology and intramammary therapy.
- Group-level treatment effects do not quantify individual ecological stability.
- Define ecological resistance explicitly and distinguish it from antimicrobial resistance and ecological resilience.
- Rationale for within-cow quarter-level reanalysis.

## Objectives
1. Quantify quarter-level ecological resistance.
2. Identify baseline ecological characteristics associated with resistance using within-cow untreated controls.
3. Characterize the dimensions of mammary ecological resistance.

## Results placeholders
1. Cohort reconstruction and ASV QC.
2. Individual baseline-relative displacement.
3. Within-cow treatment vs untreated excess displacement.
4. Baseline ecological correlates.
5. Multidimensional structure of resistance.

## Discussion
- Biological heterogeneity hidden by group-centroid analyses.
- Physiological transition vs treatment-associated perturbation.
- Implications for microbiome-sparing mastitis therapeutics.
- Low biomass and absence of extraction controls.
- Small discovery cohort; need external validation.
